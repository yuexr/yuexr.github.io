About the odds & prices

Base drip roll: 10% (1 in 10 eligible catches).
Wishbone upgrades raise this to 25%. Specific drip fish: its catch chance × your drip roll.

How the odds work

No island weighting is applied by the catch-selection method. Boss-only baits pause while a boss is active; boss outcomes from mixed pools fall back to Cod. Prices are unmodified base values. Render lighting/materials are simplified; models are scaled to fit. Manta, Albatross and Mutated Bowhead Whale have no rod-pool percentage.
