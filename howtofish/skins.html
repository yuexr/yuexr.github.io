How the odds work & asset sources

0% means no normal drop at that island. Defaults do not drop. Assault Rifle Orange, Assault Rifle Blue, Boat Fire, Pistol Missing and Fishing Rod Gold also have no normal slot-machine drop. Other zeroes mean the collection is unavailable at that machine.

Each eligible collection has equal chance, followed by an equal choice among its skins of the selected rarity. Specific skin chance = rarity probability ÷ eligible collections ÷ skins of that rarity in the collection. Duplicates can drop; ownership does not trigger a reroll. Accepted creature or trophy type, value and size do not change these odds.

Visuals: reconstructed from the original game meshes, palette and recovered skin shader calculations. These are not in-game screenshots. Lighting and reflections are simplified; optional attachments, motors, upgrades and animation are omitted.

Sources: sharedassets0.assets (10 SkinPreset collections, meshes and Colors palette); Managed/Assembly-CSharp.dll (SlotMachineManager.RollRandom, SkinPreset.GetRandomSkinIndex and unlock logic); level1–level5 and Mini1 machine settings. Island numbers follow internal scene names. Bow skins drop only at Mini1. Values are rounded to four decimal places and apply to installed build 25604825, examined 30 September 2026, with normal spins and no developer override.
