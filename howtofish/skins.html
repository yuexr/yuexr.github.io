How the odds work & asset sources

0% means no normal drop at that island. Defaults do not drop. Assault Rifle Orange, Assault Rifle Blue, Boat Fire and Pistol Missing also have no normal slot-machine drop. Other zeroes mean the collection is unavailable at that machine.

Each eligible collection has equal chance, followed by an equal choice among its skins of the selected rarity. Specific skin chance = rarity probability ÷ eligible collections ÷ skins of that rarity in the collection. Duplicates can drop; ownership does not trigger a reroll. Accepted creature or trophy type, value and size do not change these odds.

Sources: sharedassets0.assets (SkinPreset objects 7838–7846, meshes and Colors palette); Managed/Assembly-CSharp.dll (SlotMachineManager.RollRandom, SkinPreset.GetRandomSkinIndex and unlock logic); level1–level5 machine settings. Island numbers follow internal scene names. Values are rounded to four decimal places and apply to the installed build examined on 5 September 2026, with normal spins and no developer override.
